# Onboarding: Your First Weeks

## Your first day
Come to the reception on the 3rd floor at 9:30. Your laptop is ready on your desk, already set up with your company account. Your manager will introduce you to your "onboarding buddy", a colleague from another team who answers your everyday questions during your first 3 months.

## Mandatory training
Complete the security awareness training within your first 2 weeks. It takes about 45 minutes and you find it in the learning platform. The data protection (GDPR) training must be finished within your first month.

## Benefits enrollment
You have 30 days from your start date to enroll in the health insurance and the meal voucher program in Brightwave People. If you miss this deadline, you have to wait for the next enrollment period in January.

## Probation period
The probation period is 90 days. Halfway through, you have a check-in meeting with your manager. At the end, you both confirm that the role is a good fit.

## Who to ask
- Questions about pay, contracts or leave: the HR team (hr@brightwave.example).
- Problems with your laptop, accounts or access: the IT helpdesk (extension 4400).
- Everything else: your onboarding buddy.
