# Leave and Holidays

## Paid annual leave
Every full-time employee at Brightwave gets 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, on top of public holidays. Part-time employees get a share of this based on their contract (for example, 80% time gives 21.6 days, rounded up to 22).
Leave is requested in the HR portal ("Brightwave People") at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than three days. Your manager must approve it.

## Carrying over unused days
You can carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before March 31; after that date they are lost. Days cannot be exchanged for money, except when you leave the company.

## Sick leave
If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. You do not need a doctor's note for the first 3 days. From the 4th day of sick leave, upload a doctor's note to Brightwave People.
Sick days do not reduce your annual leave.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver gets 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time during the child's first year. Tell HR at least 8 weeks before the leave starts.

## Company closure
The office is closed between December 24 and January 1. These days are given by the company and are not taken from your 27 days.
