# IT and Security

## Signing in (SSO)
All company applications use single sign-on (SSO) through the Brightwave identity provider. You sign in once with your company email and you get access to the apps you need. Never create a separate password for a company app. If an app asks for a different login, report it to the IT helpdesk.

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every employee. Use an authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS codes are not accepted because they can be stolen by SIM swapping.

## Passwords
Your password must be at least 14 characters long. We recommend a passphrase made of four or more random words. You do not have to change your password regularly; change it only if you think someone else knows it. Never reuse your company password on another website.

## Lost or stolen devices
If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour, by phone on extension 4400 or at helpdesk@brightwave.example. IT will lock the device remotely and erase it if needed.

## Phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click the links. Use the "Report phishing" button in your email client. The security team checks every report within one business day. Reporting a real phishing email is never a mistake, even if you clicked the link by accident.

## USB drives and personal devices
Personal USB drives cannot be used on company laptops. To share large files, use the company file storage. Personal phones may access company email only through the official mobile app.
