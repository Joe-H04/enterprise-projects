# Travel and Expenses

## How to submit an expense
Submit every expense in the "Spendly" app within 30 days of paying. Take a photo of the receipt; expenses without a receipt are not reimbursed, except for amounts under €15 such as a metro ticket. Approved expenses are paid back with your next salary or within 10 business days, whichever comes first.

## Approval before you spend
Any expense above €500 (for example a flight or a conference ticket) needs written approval from your manager before you book it. Expenses above €2,000 also need approval from the Finance department.

## Meals
The meal limit is €40 per day when travelling in your home country and €60 per day when travelling abroad. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Team dinners organized by a manager have their own budget and are not counted in your daily limit.

## Transport
Take the train for any trip shorter than 4 hours. For longer trips you may fly, in economy class only. Taxis are allowed late at night (after 22:00) or when you carry heavy equipment. If you use your own car, you are paid €0.35 per kilometre.

## Hotels
Book hotels through the company travel portal. The limit is €150 per night in most cities and €220 per night in Paris, London and New York.
