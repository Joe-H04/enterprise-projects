# Remote Work Policy

## How many days can I work from home?
Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams choose at least one shared "office day" each week so everyone meets in person. New employees work from the office every day during their first 4 weeks.

## Core hours
Wherever you work, you must be reachable during core hours: 10:00 to 16:00 (Paris time), Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can organize your time freely, as long as your weekly hours match your contract.

## Working from another country
You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per year. You need written approval from HR before you travel, because of tax and insurance rules. Working from a country under sanctions is never allowed.

## Home office allowance
Brightwave pays a one-time home office allowance of €300 for a chair, a desk or a screen, and €25 per month towards your internet bill. Claim both through the expense app (see the Expenses policy). Keep the receipts.

## Security when working remotely
Always connect through the company VPN when you use internal tools from outside the office. Do not work on confidential documents in public places where others can read your screen.
